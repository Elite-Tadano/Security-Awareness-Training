Security Awareness Exercise

⚠ Don't worry — your real Aatman account is untouched. This was a simulated phishing exercise conducted by the Information Security Team.

Thank you for participating.

You just entered your credentials into a simulated Aatman Wellness portal login page, reached via a fake "Desktop Support" email about an expiring password. This exercise is part of Aurus Tech Pvt Ltd's ongoing Security Awareness Program and is intended to help employees recognize credential-harvesting attempts before they happen for real.

🕵️ Plot twist: there is no password expiry policy on the Aatman portal, and the Desktop Team doesn't email you links to "update your credentials." What you actually updated today was your instinct to double-check before typing a password into a page you clicked into from an email.

What could have happened in a real attack?

  • Your real Aatman (or corporate) password stolen and reused elsewhere
  • Account takeover of your wellness or corporate profile
  • Attackers pivoting into other systems using the same password
  • Unauthorized access to personal health and benefits data
  • Financial fraud or business email compromise

Warning signs in this email and page

  • Unsolicited "password expiry" notice from IT/Desktop Support
  • Urgency: a 24-hour deadline with a threat of losing access
  • A link taking you straight to a login form asking for your current AND new password
  • A look-alike portal that isn't the actual Aatman domain
  • Pressure to act immediately without verifying through official channels

What should you do next time?

  • Never enter your password after clicking a link in an unexpected email — go to the portal directly by typing the URL yourself, or use your saved bookmark.
  • Check the actual domain in the address bar before entering any credentials.
  • Verify "password expiry" or "account lock" claims with IT through a known, official channel.
  • Report suspicious emails immediately instead of acting on them.
